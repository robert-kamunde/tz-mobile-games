# Asset Inventory

Since 0.20.0 the character faces are picture files in `packages/pool/portraits/` (since 0.21.0 Robert's ChatGPT pictures; prompts in `PORTRAIT_PROMPTS.md`); since 0.31.0 the title logo, menu background and app icon are Robert's ChatGPT pictures in `packages/pool/art/` (`logo.png`, `menu-background.jpg`, `icon.jpg`; prompts in `ART_PROMPTS.md`, originals in `art/source/`), replacing the drawn ones, which stay as the fallback. There are no other art, audio or font files. Since 0.10.0 the table, balls, cue and table controls, and since 0.17.0 the portraits, buttons, panels, logo and menu background, are "Final v1 (Claude)": drawn by the game at start-up from `config/art.ts` (A24), good enough to ship, and a designer may replace any of them with an image under the same texture key. Since 0.19.0 the app icon, splash screens and store pictures are drawn by the same code, and the on-screen "placeholder art" labels are gone. Sounds are synthesised (version 2, 0.19.0); recordings may replace them. The font is still the system font.
