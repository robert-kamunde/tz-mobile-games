Sera ya faragha ya Bongo Pool Table

Imesasishwa: 11 Oktoba 2026

Bongo Pool Table ("mchezo") imetengenezwa na FAR Data Solutions. Sera hii inaeleza jinsi mchezo unavyoshughulikia taarifa zako.

Hatukusanyi taarifa zako. Mchezo hautumii akaunti, hauombi jina, namba ya simu, barua pepe wala mahali ulipo, na hautumi chochote kwenye intaneti.

Kinachohifadhiwa kwenye simu yako tu: mchezo unaoendelea, takwimu zako (michezo uliyocheza na kushinda), lugha uliyochagua na kama sauti imewashwa. Taarifa hizi zinabaki kwenye simu yako. Ukifuta data ya programu au kuiondoa, zinafutika. Huduma ya Android ya kuhifadhi nakala (backup) inaweza kuziweka kwenye akaunti yako ya Google kama umeiwasha; hiyo inasimamiwa na Google.

Matangazo na malipo: toleo hili halina matangazo wala malipo ndani ya mchezo.

Watoto: mchezo haukusanyi taarifa za mtu yeyote, wakiwemo watoto.

Mabadiliko: tukibadilisha sera hii (kwa mfano tukiongeza matangazo au mchezo wa mtandaoni), tutasasisha ukurasa huu kabla ya toleo hilo kutoka.

English

Bongo Pool Table privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Bongo Pool Table ("the game") is made by FAR Data Solutions. This policy explains how the game handles your information.

We do not collect your data. The game has no account, does not ask for your name, phone number, email or location, and sends nothing over the internet.

Stored only on your phone: the game in progress, your stats (games played and won), your chosen language and whether sound is on. This stays on your phone. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes it. Android's backup service may copy it to your Google account if you have turned that on; that is managed by Google.

Ads and purchases: this version has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Children: the game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes: if this policy changes (for example if ads or online play are added), this page will be updated before that version is released.
